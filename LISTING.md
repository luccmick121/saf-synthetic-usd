## Descrição curta (EN) — cole no campo "Description"
SAF Synthetic USD (sUSD) is a TRC-20 synthetic asset on TRON with on-chain governance metadata.
Its liquidity is seeded 1:1 against USDT on SunSwap V2, so the pool opens at exactly US$1.000000.
Token metadata (name, symbol, characteristics, contract URI) is updatable on-chain by the owner,
with every change emitting an event. Transfer governance supports an anti-sale mode and an
on-chain whitelist. Fixed supply of 100,000,000,000 — no public mint.

## Descrição curta (PT-BR)
sUSD é um ativo sintético TRC-20 na TRON, com liquidez semeada 1:1 em USDT na SunSwap V2
